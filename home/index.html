Featured: knead.it

I am the founder and principal engineer of knead.it, a cross-platform cooking education platform. knead.it teaches technique through voice-first lessons, gives every cook a personal recipe library, and maps shared recipes to the places they come from on an interactive culinary globe. The platform is engineered end-to-end from a single codebase for web, iOS, and Android, and is developed through an organization of specialized AI agents of my own design, with evaluation and quality gates on every release.

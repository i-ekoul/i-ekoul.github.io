Bio

I did not come to software the usual way. Before engineering I worked with words: editing, technical writing, and translation across subjects as far apart as laser optics and financial markets. I earned my computer science degree while already shipping production software, and the editorial years never really left. I write things down, I care about how a decision gets documented as much as what gets decided, and I hold anything user-facing to the same standard an editor would.

What pulled me into this field, and keeps me here, is a fascination with how things fit together: systems, teams, and the tools that shape both. I like the unglamorous parts of engineering that most people skip, the decision records, the postmortems, the test that catches the regression nobody predicted. Give me an ambiguous problem and the freedom to structure it, and I am at my happiest.

Originally from Southern California, now based in Cedar Park, Texas, I have lived enough places to know that the way people cook, play, and tell stories says more about them than any title does. That belief is quietly behind everything on this site, including the company I founded.

Away from the keyboard you will find me outdoors or in the kitchen. Camping and cooking are my two real passions, and the second one grew into a company. The best software, like the best cooking, comes from patience, good ingredients, and knowing when to stop.
